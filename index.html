Markup guide
  • # Name then one line of contact details split with |
  • ## Section starts a section (Summary, Education, Skills, Experience, Projects)
  • ### Title | Detail | Date starts an entry; the last part sits on the right
  • - text is a bullet; Label: a, b is a skills line; **bold** works anywhere

AI settings

    
AI review, tailoring and import use the Anthropic API with your own key. The key is stored only in this browser and requests go straight from your browser to Anthropic. Use a key with a spend limit, and do not save it on a shared computer.
